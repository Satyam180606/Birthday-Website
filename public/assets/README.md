# Photo & Audio Assets Directory

Drop your girlfriend's photos and your couple's song into this folder!
Once you add the files with the matching names below, the website will automatically display them with your customized captions.

## Image Files:
- `birthday-hero.jpg` — The main opening hero photograph
- `birthday-portrait.jpg` — Large photo for the Happy Birthday letter card
- `memory-01.jpg` to `memory-06.jpg` — Section 1: "A Little World of Us" Scrapbook gallery
- `reason-01.jpg` to `reason-06.jpg` — Section 3: "Reasons I Love You" 6 grid cards
- `song-cover.jpg` — Section 4: Album cover for your custom vinyl record player
- `word-01.jpg` to `word-03.jpg` — Section 5: "Beautiful", "Kind", "Irreplaceable" cards
- `letter-photo-01.jpg`, `letter-photo-02.jpg` — Section 6: Love Letter accent photos
- `little-thing-01.jpg` to `little-thing-04.jpg` — Section 7: "The Little Things" memory cards
- `final-photo.jpg` — Section 8: Closing scene final romantic photo

## Music File:
- `our-song.mp3` — Your couple's song! The vinyl record player will stream this audio file. If not added yet, the player displays a graceful placeholder: *"Our song will play here ♥"*.

All captions, names, dates, and text can be edited directly in `src/data/loveStory.ts`.
